About Us

Professional. Genuine. People First.

MKW Solutions & Services Hub

MKW Solutions & Services Hub is a PNG-based consultancy company committed to providing professional, genuine, and accessible services to individuals, families, and SMEs across Papua New Guinea.


Founded in Morobe Province, MKW was built on three core pillars to support growth, health, and opportunity for Papua New Guineans.


We currently serve clients in Lae, Port Moresby, Manus, Kimbe and Mt Hagen, with plans to expand nationwide.

Our 3 Core Pillars

Services designed to help people travel, stay well, and build successful businesses.

✈️

1. Travel Booking Agent

Domestic and international flight bookings, tour packages, accommodation, and travel consultation.

We aim to make travel simple, affordable, and less stressful for business and leisure clients.

❤️

2. Healthcare Solutions

Working with trusted brands and distributors to provide health and wellness products, education, and support.

Our goal is to support individuals and communities in making informed wellness choices.

💼

3. Business & Accounting Services

Supporting SMEs and startups with business registration, system setup, accounting, marketing, and growth support.

From idea to operation, we help clients build stronger foundations for business.
