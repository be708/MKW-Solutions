About Us

Supporting people, families and businesses across Papua New Guinea.

MKW Solutions & Services Hub is a PNG-based consultancy company committed to providing professional, genuine and accessible services to individuals, families and SMEs across Papua New Guinea.

Founded in Morobe Province, MKW was built on three core pillars to support growth, health and opportunity for Papua New Guineans.

We currently serve clients in Lae, Port Moresby, Manus, Kimbe and Mt Hagen, with plans to expand nationwide.

Our 3 Core Pillars

Professional services designed to help PNG people and businesses grow.

✈️

Travel Booking Agent

Domestic and international flight bookings, tour packages, accommodation and travel consultation.

We aim to make travel simple, affordable and stress-free for business and leisure clients.

❤️

Healthcare Solutions

Providing health and wellness products, education and support through trusted partners and distributors.

We support individuals and communities with access to health and wellness information and products.

💼

Business & Accounting Services

Helping SMEs and startups register, establish systems, manage accounts and grow their businesses.

From business registration to marketing support, we help turn ideas into action.
